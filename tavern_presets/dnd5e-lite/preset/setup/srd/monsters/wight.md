# wight

*Medium undead, CR 3 (700 XP)*

- **Armor Class:** 14
- **Hit Points:** 45 (6d8+18)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 15 · DEX 14 · CON 16 · INT 10 · WIS 13 · CHA 15
- **Saving Throws:** —
- **Skills:** perception, stealth
- **Damage Resistances:** piercing, bludgeoning, slashing, necrotic
- **Damage Immunities:** poison
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft.
- **Languages:** the languages it knew in life
- **Challenge:** 3

## Traits
- **Sunlight Sensitivity:** While in sunlight, the wight has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

## Actions
- **life-drain** (melee): +4 to hit, 1d6+2 necrotic damage, 5 ft.
- **longsword** (melee): +4 to hit, 1d8+2 slashing damage, 5 ft.
- **longbow** (ranged): +4 to hit, 1d8+2 piercing damage
