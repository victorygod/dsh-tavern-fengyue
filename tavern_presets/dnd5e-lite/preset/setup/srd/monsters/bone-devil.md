# bone-devil

*Large fiend, CR 9 (5000 XP)*

- **Armor Class:** 19
- **Hit Points:** 142 (15d10+60)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 18 · DEX 16 · CON 18 · INT 13 · WIS 14 · CHA 16
- **Saving Throws:** int, wis, cha
- **Skills:** deception, insight
- **Damage Resistances:** piercing, bludgeoning, slashing, cold
- **Damage Immunities:** fire, poison
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
- **Senses:** darkvision 120 ft.
- **Languages:** Infernal, telepathy 120 ft.
- **Challenge:** 9

## Traits
- **Devil's Sight:** Magical darkness doesn't impede the devil's darkvision.
- **Magic Resistance:** The devil has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions
- **claw** (melee): +8 to hit, 1d8+4 slashing damage, 10 ft.
- **sting** (melee): +8 to hit, 2d8+4 piercing damage, 10 ft. plus 5d6 poison; on hit target must succeed on a DC 14 constitution saving throw or be poisoned (1 minute)
