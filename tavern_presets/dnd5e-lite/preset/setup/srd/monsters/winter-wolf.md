# winter-wolf

*Large monstrosity, CR 3 (700 XP)*

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Hit Points:** 75 (10d10+20)
- **Speed:** 50 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 18 · DEX 13 · CON 14 · INT 7 · WIS 12 · CHA 8
- **Saving Throws:** —
- **Skills:** perception, stealth
- **Damage Resistances:** —
- **Damage Immunities:** cold
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
- **Languages:** Common, Giant, Winter Wolf
- **Challenge:** 3

## Traits
- **Keen Hearing and Smell:** The wolf has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.
- **Pack Tactics:** The wolf has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the wolf's allies is within 5 ft. of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.
- **Snow Camouflage:** The wolf has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in snowy terrain.

## Actions
- **bite** (melee): +6 to hit, 2d6+4 piercing damage, 5 ft.; on hit target must succeed on a DC 14 strength saving throw or be prone

## Special Abilities
- **cold-breath**: dexterity saving throw DC 12, 4d8 cold damage
