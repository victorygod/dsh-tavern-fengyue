# ancient-white-dragon

*Gargantuan dragon, CR 20 (25000 XP)*

- **Armor Class:** 20
- **Hit Points:** 333 (18d20+144)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 26 · DEX 10 · CON 26 · INT 10 · WIS 13 · CHA 14
- **Saving Throws:** dex, con, wis, cha
- **Skills:** perception, stealth
- **Damage Resistances:** —
- **Damage Immunities:** cold
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
- **Senses:** darkvision 120 ft.
- **Languages:** Common, Draconic
- **Challenge:** 20

## Traits
- **Ice Walk:** The dragon can move across and climb icy surfaces without needing to make an ability check. Additionally, difficult terrain composed of ice or snow doesn't cost it extra movement.
- **Legendary Resistance:** If the dragon fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

## Actions
- **bite** (melee): +14 to hit, 2d10+8 piercing damage, 15 ft. plus 2d8 cold
- **claw** (melee): +14 to hit, 2d6+8 slashing damage, 10 ft.
- **tail** (melee): +14 to hit, 2d8+8 bludgeoning damage, 20 ft.

## Special Abilities
- **cold-breath**: constitution saving throw DC 22, 16d8 cold damage
- **wing-attack-costs-2-actions-legendary-**: dexterity saving throw DC 22, 2d6+8 bludgeoning damage, no damage on save, knocks prone
