- **Armor Class:** 14

- **Speed:** 60 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 19 · DEX 16 · CON 14 · INT 7 · WIS 14 · CHA 10
- **Saving Throws:** —
- **Skills:** perception
- **Damage Resistances:** —
- **Damage Immunities:** —
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
- **Languages:** Giant Elk, understands Common, Elvish, and Sylvan but can't speak

## Traits
- **Charge:** If the elk moves at least 20 ft. straight toward a target and then hits it with a ram attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

## Actions
- **ram** (melee): +6 to hit, 2d6+4 bludgeoning damage, 10 ft.
- **hooves** (melee): +6 to hit, 4d8+4 bludgeoning damage, 5 ft.
