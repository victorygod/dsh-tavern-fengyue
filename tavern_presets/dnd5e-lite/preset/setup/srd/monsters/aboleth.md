# aboleth

*Large aberration, CR 10 (5900 XP)*

- **Armor Class:** 17
- **Hit Points:** 135 (18d10+36)
- **Speed:** 10 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 21 · DEX 9 · CON 15 · INT 18 · WIS 15 · CHA 18
- **Saving Throws:** con, int, wis
- **Skills:** history, perception
- **Damage Resistances:** —
- **Damage Immunities:** —
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
- **Senses:** darkvision 120 ft.
- **Languages:** Deep Speech, telepathy 120 ft.
- **Challenge:** 10

## Traits
- **Amphibious:** The aboleth can breathe air and water.
- **Mucous Cloud:** While underwater, the aboleth is surrounded by transformative mucus. A creature that touches the aboleth or that hits it with a melee attack while within 5 ft. of it must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the creature is diseased for 1d4 hours. The diseased creature can breathe only underwater.
- **Probing Telepathy:** If a creature communicates telepathically with the aboleth, the aboleth learns the creature's greatest desires if the aboleth can see the creature.

## Actions
- **tentacle** (melee): +9 to hit, 2d6+5 bludgeoning damage, 10 ft.
- **tail** (melee): +9 to hit, 3d6+5 bludgeoning damage, 10 ft.
