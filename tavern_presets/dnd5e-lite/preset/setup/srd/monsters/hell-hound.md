# hell-hound

*Medium fiend, CR 3 (700 XP)*

- **Armor Class:** 15
- **Hit Points:** 45 (7d8+14)
- **Speed:** 50 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 17 · DEX 12 · CON 14 · INT 6 · WIS 13 · CHA 6
- **Saving Throws:** —
- **Skills:** perception
- **Damage Resistances:** —
- **Damage Immunities:** fire
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft.
- **Languages:** understands Infernal but can't speak it
- **Challenge:** 3

## Traits
- **Keen Hearing and Smell:** The hound has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.
- **Pack Tactics:** The hound has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the hound's allies is within 5 ft. of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

## Actions
- **bite** (melee): +5 to hit, 1d8+3 piercing damage, 5 ft. plus 2d6 fire

## Special Abilities
- **fire-breath**: dexterity saving throw DC 12, 6d6 fire damage
