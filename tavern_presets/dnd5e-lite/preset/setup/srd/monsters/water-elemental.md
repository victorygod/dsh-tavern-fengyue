# water-elemental

*Large elemental, CR 5 (1800 XP)*

- **Armor Class:** 14
- **Hit Points:** 114 (12d10+48)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 18 · DEX 14 · CON 18 · INT 5 · WIS 10 · CHA 8
- **Saving Throws:** —
- **Skills:** —
- **Damage Resistances:** piercing, bludgeoning, slashing, acid
- **Damage Immunities:** poison
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft.
- **Languages:** Aquan
- **Challenge:** 5

## Traits
- **Water Form:** The elemental can enter a hostile creature's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.
- **Freeze:** If the elemental takes cold damage, it partially freezes; its speed is reduced by 20 ft. until the end of its next turn.

## Actions
- **slam** (melee): +7 to hit, 2d8+4 bludgeoning damage, 5 ft.
