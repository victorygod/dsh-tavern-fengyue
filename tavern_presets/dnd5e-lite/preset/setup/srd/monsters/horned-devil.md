- **Speed:** 20 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 22 · DEX 17 · CON 21 · INT 12 · WIS 16 · CHA 17
- **Saving Throws:** str, dex, wis, cha
- **Skills:** —

- **Damage Immunities:** fire, poison
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
