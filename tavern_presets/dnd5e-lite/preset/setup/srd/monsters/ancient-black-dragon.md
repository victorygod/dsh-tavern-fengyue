- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 27 · DEX 14 · CON 25 · INT 16 · WIS 15 · CHA 19
- **Saving Throws:** dex, con, wis, cha
- **Skills:** perception, stealth
- **Damage Resistances:** —
