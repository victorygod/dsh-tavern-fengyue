# erinyes

*Medium fiend, CR 12 (8400 XP)*

- **Armor Class:** 18
- **Hit Points:** 153 (18d8+72)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 18 · DEX 16 · CON 18 · INT 14 · WIS 14 · CHA 18
- **Saving Throws:** dex, con, wis, cha
- **Skills:** —
- **Damage Resistances:** piercing, bludgeoning, slashing, cold
- **Damage Immunities:** fire, poison
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
- **Languages:** Infernal, telepathy 120 ft.
- **Challenge:** 12

## Traits
- **Hellish Weapons:** The erinyes's weapon attacks are magical and deal an extra 13 (3d8) poison damage on a hit (included in the attacks).
- **Magic Resistance:** The erinyes has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions
- **longsword** (melee): +8 to hit, 1d8+4 slashing damage, 5 ft. plus 3d8 poison
- **longbow** (ranged): +7 to hit, 1d8+3 piercing damage plus 3d8 poison; on hit target must succeed on a DC 14 constitution saving throw or be poisoned
