# ice-devil

*Large fiend, CR 14 (11500 XP)*

- **Armor Class:** 18
- **Hit Points:** 180 (19d10+76)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 21 · DEX 14 · CON 18 · INT 18 · WIS 15 · CHA 18
- **Saving Throws:** dex, con, wis, cha
- **Skills:** —
- **Damage Resistances:** piercing, bludgeoning, slashing
- **Damage Immunities:** fire, poison
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
- **Senses:** darkvision 120 ft.
- **Languages:** Infernal, telepathy 120 ft.
- **Challenge:** 14

## Traits
- **Devil's Sight:** Magical darkness doesn't impede the devil's darkvision.
- **Magic Resistance:** The devil has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions
- **bite** (melee): +10 to hit, 2d6+5 piercing damage, 5 ft. plus 3d6 cold
- **claws** (melee): +10 to hit, 2d4+5 slashing damage, 5 ft. plus 3d6 cold
- **tail** (melee): +10 to hit, 2d6+5 bludgeoning damage, 10 ft. plus 3d6 cold
