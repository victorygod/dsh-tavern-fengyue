# kraken

*Gargantuan monstrosity, CR 23 (50000 XP)*

- **Armor Class:** 18
- **Hit Points:** 472 (27d20+189)
- **Speed:** 20 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 30 · DEX 11 · CON 25 · INT 22 · WIS 18 · CHA 20
- **Saving Throws:** str, dex, con, int, wis
- **Skills:** —
- **Damage Resistances:** —
- **Damage Immunities:** piercing, bludgeoning, slashing, lightning
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
- **Languages:** understands Abyssal, Celestial, Infernal, and Primordial but can't speak, telepathy 120 ft.
- **Challenge:** 23

## Traits
- **Amphibious:** The kraken can breathe air and water.
- **Freedom of Movement:** The kraken ignores difficult terrain, and magical effects can't reduce its speed or cause it to be restrained. It can spend 5 feet of movement to escape from nonmagical restraints or being grappled.
- **Siege Monster:** The kraken deals double damage to objects and structures.

## Actions
- **bite** (melee): +7 to hit, 3d8+10 piercing damage, 5 ft.
- **tentacle** (melee): +7 to hit, 3d6+10 bludgeoning damage, 30 ft.
