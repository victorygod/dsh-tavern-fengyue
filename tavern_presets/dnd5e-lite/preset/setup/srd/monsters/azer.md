# azer

*Medium elemental, CR 2 (450 XP)*

- **Armor Class:** 15
- **Hit Points:** 39 (6d8+12)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 17 · DEX 12 · CON 15 · INT 12 · WIS 13 · CHA 10
- **Saving Throws:** con
- **Skills:** —
- **Damage Resistances:** —
- **Damage Immunities:** fire, poison
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
- **Languages:** Ignan
- **Challenge:** 2

## Traits
- **Heated Body:** A creature that touches the azer or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 ft. of it takes 5 (1d10) fire damage.
- **Heated Weapons:** When the azer hits with a metal melee weapon, it deals an extra 3 (1d6) fire damage (included in the attack).
- **Illumination:** The azer sheds bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 ft..

## Actions
- **warhammer** (melee): +5 to hit, 1d8+3 bludgeoning damage, 5 ft. plus 1d6 fire
