# wereboar-human

*Medium humanoid, CR 4 (1100 XP)*

- **Armor Class:** 10
- **Hit Points:** 78 (12d8+24)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 17 · DEX 10 · CON 15 · INT 10 · WIS 11 · CHA 8
- **Saving Throws:** —
- **Skills:** perception
- **Damage Resistances:** —
- **Damage Immunities:** piercing, bludgeoning, slashing
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
- **Languages:** Common (can't speak in boar form)
- **Challenge:** 4

## Traits
- **Shapechanger:** The wereboar can use its action to polymorph into a boar-humanoid hybrid or into a boar, or back into its true form, which is humanoid. Its statistics, other than its AC, are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed. It reverts to its true form if it dies.
- **Relentless:** If the wereboar takes 14 damage or less that would reduce it to 0 hit points, it is reduced to 1 hit point instead.
