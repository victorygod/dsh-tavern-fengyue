# gladiator

*Medium humanoid, CR 5 (1800 XP)*

- **Armor Class:** 16
- **Hit Points:** 112 (15d8+45)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 18 · DEX 15 · CON 16 · INT 10 · WIS 12 · CHA 15
- **Saving Throws:** str, dex, con
- **Skills:** athletics, intimidation
- **Damage Resistances:** —
- **Damage Immunities:** —
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** —
- **Languages:** any one language (usually Common)
- **Challenge:** 5

## Traits
- **Brave:** The gladiator has advantage on saving throws against being frightened.
- **Brute:** A melee weapon deals one extra die of its damage when the gladiator hits with it (included in the attack).

## Actions
- **shield-bash** (melee): +7 to hit, 2d4+4 bludgeoning damage, 5 ft.; on hit target must succeed on a DC 15 strength saving throw or be prone
