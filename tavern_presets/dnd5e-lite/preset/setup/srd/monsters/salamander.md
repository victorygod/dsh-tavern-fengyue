# salamander

*Large elemental, CR 5 (1800 XP)*

- **Armor Class:** 15
- **Hit Points:** 90 (12d10+24)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 18 · DEX 14 · CON 15 · INT 11 · WIS 10 · CHA 12
- **Saving Throws:** —
- **Skills:** —
- **Damage Resistances:** piercing, bludgeoning, slashing
- **Damage Immunities:** fire
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** cold
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft.
- **Languages:** Ignan
- **Challenge:** 5

## Traits
- **Heated Body:** A creature that touches the salamander or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 ft. of it takes 7 (2d6) fire damage.
- **Heated Weapons:** Any metal melee weapon the salamander wields deals an extra 3 (1d6) fire damage on a hit (included in the attack).

## Actions
- **tail** (melee): +7 to hit, 2d6+4 bludgeoning damage, 10 ft. plus 2d6 fire
